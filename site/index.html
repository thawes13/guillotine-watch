How this is calculated

The rest of the week is simulated times. Points already scored are locked in; points still to come are drawn at random around ESPN's projection, using how far real results have landed from projections in this league so far this season.

A team's chance is the share of simulations in which it finished last. "Likely range" is the middle 80% of its simulated final scores.

It assumes each manager will end up with their best-projected lineup: a bench player whose game hasn't started is assumed to replace a starter he out-projects by more than a point. Players whose games have kicked off are locked in place. Any assumed change is shown on the team's row.

Players are treated as independent of one another.
